# Economy Balance Notes (Phase 14)

## Core Philosophy
The economy is designed to provide a rewarding sense of progression where basic operations are sustainable, but facility expansion and high-tier species require strategic saving and reputation building.

## Income Streams
- **Common Adoptions**: $100 - $150. Reliable baseline.
- **Rare Adoptions**: $600 - $900. Significant boosts for high-quality care.
- **Legendary Windfalls**: $3000+. Major milestones that allow for large facility transformations.

## Progression Milestones (Projected)

### Day 1-3: The Dog Era
- **Goal**: Establish basic operations.
- **Earnings**: ~$300 - $450 total.
- **Key Purchase**: Vet Triage Corner ($75) on Day 3.
- **Status**: Sustainable, allows for small shop purchases (e.g., Premium Bowl $30).

### Day 4-7: The Expansion Choice
- **Capacity vs. Species**: The the player will earn ~$600-$900 more.
- **Option A**: Kennel Expansion ($250). Increases daily earnings potential.
- **Option B**: Species Path (Cats). Requires Exam Room ($150) + Cat habitat ($300) = $450.
- **Choice**: Provides a meaningful decision point. Most players will likely expand capacity first, then save for cats.

### Day 10+: The Expert Era
- **Scaling**: With multiple kennel slots and cat adoptions, daily income reaches $500+.
- **Higher Tiers**: Hutch ($450) and Aviary ($700) become reachable goals.
- **Reputation**: DOG 3 adoptions -> CAT 6 adoptions -> SMALL ANIMAL. Ensures player spends roughly 3-5 days in each tier.
