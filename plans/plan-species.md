### 2. **Temperature Regulation** (Turtles, Tortoises)
**Morning Board Call:** *"A turtle was found too far from water. They're too cold to move quickly. We need to warm them up safely."*

**Location:** Riverbank or pond edge

**Mechanic:** The turtle is on the bank, moving very slowly. You have a heat lamp and a thermometer. You must position the heat lamp at the correct distance (not too close, not too far) to warm the turtle gradually. A temperature meter shows progress. Too fast = turtle retreats. Correct pace = turtle becomes active and healthy.

**Background needed:** Reuse existing riverside or create pond edge variant

### 4. **Temperature Crisis** (Tropical Fish)
**Morning Board Call:** *"Tank heater broke overnight. Tropical fish are lethargic from cold. Need to warm water gradually."*

**Location:** Fish store or home

**Mechanic:** A temperature gauge shows water is too cold. You must add small amounts of warm water (not hot!) to raise temperature slowly. A meter shows water temp. Add too fast = temperature shock. Add too slowly = fish stay stressed. Find the right pace to gradually warm water to safe range.

### 5. **Bubble Counter** (Betta Fish)
**Morning Board Call:** *"A betta in a cup at the store looks stressed. We should check oxygen levels and get them to a better space."*

**Location:** Pet store

Too few = fish still stressed. Too many = water too turbulent. Get it just right and the betta perks up.
